Prototype disclaimer: Doses shown are calculated from published AHA/AAP 2025 PALS guideline and emergency airway reference values, not an institution-specific protocol. This is a design prototype only — not validated for clinical use, not a substitute for institutional order sets, pharmacist verification, or clinical judgment. A production version would load your facility's P&T-approved dosing protocol rather than these reference defaults.

Weight estimation sourcing: Length-based estimates use published mean measured-weight data per Broselow color zone (peer-reviewed validation study, 2017-edition tape). Estimates beyond the tape's validated range (>146.5 cm) use an age-based formula validated in UK/Australian pediatric populations — this fallback is not an AHA/AAP-endorsed US standard and is offered only as a labeled, secondary estimate pending actual measured weight.

Event log: Doses you log are saved only on this device (browser local storage) — never sent anywhere — and are cleared when you start a new event. It's a scratch timer/log for this prototype, not a substitute for your institution's official code documentation or the medical record. Cumulative-dose warnings are currently implemented only for drugs with a published cumulative ceiling (e.g. amiodarone's 15 mg/kg total); absence of a warning for another drug does not mean no ceiling exists.
